I'm aware that having a non-responsive portfolio is not the most optimal choice…
but I think that this, just like my projects, can also say something about me.

I like that this website has the ability to interrupt the continuous flow of things that constantly pass in front of us.

I like simple things, and analyzing complex ones until I can see their simplicity.
That's why, in its construction as well, I wanted to keep this website as simple as possible, not only in its matter, but also in its form.*

I like how, even as an outsider to the field, I was able to build my own small space from nothing.

and I like the idea that there are very precise conditions, and to remind myself that in design these conditions are always set by me, whether I do it consciously or not.

*matter and form refer to Vilém Flusser's definitions in The Shape of Things: A Philosophy of Design
